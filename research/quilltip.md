## Descripción del producto

QuillTip is a decentralized publishing platform on Stellar that empowers content creators with direct reader tipping, NFT-backed ownership, and censorship-resistant distribution. It connects writers/creators with readers who pay for content or tips.

---

## Por qué es candidato para Slice

QuillTip has creators and readers exchanging value (tips, paid content, NFT ownership). Parties are often strangers. Disputes can arise over content quality, plagiarism, refund requests, or tip chargebacks. Meets at least 2 criteria: P2P value exchange and stranger transactions with subjective deliverables.
