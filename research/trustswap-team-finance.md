## Descripción del producto

TrustSwap provides the infrastructure layer for token projects, exchanges, and institutional partners to manage assets, raise capital, and reach investors. Its core product Team Finance offers token creation, liquidity locks, team token/NFT locks, vesting schedules, staking pools, and distribution tools across 26 blockchain networks. They have a Launchpad for token fundraising and The Crypto App (5.7M+ downloads). SCF-funded to bring their infrastructure to Stellar/Soroban.

---

## Por qué es candidato para Slice

TrustSwap sits at the center of token operations where multiple parties interact: token issuers, investors, liquidity providers, and vesting beneficiaries. Disputes can arise around lock terms, vesting schedule changes, fundraising commitments, and token distribution. As a multi-chain infrastructure provider with high transaction volumes, TrustSwap would benefit from Slice's automated arbitration to handle disputes without manual intervention. Meets criteria: multiple parties exchanging value, escrow-like mechanisms (locks, vesting), high transaction frequency.
