## Descripción del producto

Boundless is a decentralized crowdfunding and grants platform on the Stellar blockchain that enables transparent, milestone-based funding for projects. It connects creators and backers (or grant programs) through Trustless Work escrow, releasing funds only when milestones are verified. It supports crowdfunding campaigns, grants, hackathons, and bounties.

---

## Por qué es candidato para Slice

Boundless has two distinct parties (funders/backers and creators/contributors) exchanging value through milestone-based escrow. When a milestone is disputed (funder says not delivered, creator says delivered), there is no neutral third party to resolve the conflict. The platform itself would benefit from Slice's arbitration layer to handle these disputes without manual intervention. Boundless meets at least 3 criteria: P2P value exchange between strangers, escrowed funds requiring release conditions, and subjective deliverable verification.
