## Descripción del producto

Juntta is a Web3 crowdfunding platform focused on the Latin American market, built on the Stellar network. It enables project creators to raise funds from backers using blockchain-based infrastructure for transparency and efficient cross-border payments. It serves the LATAM region where traditional crowdfunding has high friction due to banking limitations and currency controls.

---

## Por qué es candidato para Slice

Juntta operates a classic crowdfunding model where backers fund projects and creators are expected to deliver on their promises. This creates inherent dispute scenarios: projects that don't deliver, funds that need to be returned, backers who dispute the quality of deliverables. Crowdfunding platforms traditionally handle this manually or not at all. Slice could provide automated, neutral arbitration for disputed fund releases or refunds. Meets criteria: P2P between strangers, escrowed funds, subjective deliverables.
