## Descripción del producto

GearUp is a Stellar-based P2P marketplace for African creators to rent, buy, and sell gear, studio spaces, courses, and gig opportunities. It connects buyers and sellers (or renters and owners) who often do not know each other, with payments and listings handled on-chain through the Stellar ecosystem.

---

## Por qué es candidato para Slice

GearUp has two parties exchanging value (buyers/renters and sellers/owners) who may be strangers. Transactions depend on fulfillment (item delivery, space access, course completion, gig work). There is clear bad-faith incentive (not delivering gear, misrepresenting listings, withholding payment). Meets at least 3 criteria: P2P value exchange, fulfillment-based fund release, and stranger-to-stranger transactions.
