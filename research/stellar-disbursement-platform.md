## Descripción del producto

The Stellar Disbursement Platform (SDP) is an open-source tool used by NGOs, fintechs, and grant programs to disburse funds to large numbers of recipients via Stellar. Organizations (disbursers) send money to beneficiaries (recipients). Real deployments include NGO aid disbursements and grant programs with real fund flows.

---

## Por qué es candidato para Slice

- Funds transferred from organizations to beneficiaries based on fulfillment of criteria (aid delivery, milestone completion)
- Parties include organizations and recipients who may not know each other
- Incentive for dispute: recipients claim non-receipt; disbursers dispute eligibility
- Real money moving in active deployments across multiple organizations
- Milestone-based grant disbursements can generate disputes over deliverable acceptance
