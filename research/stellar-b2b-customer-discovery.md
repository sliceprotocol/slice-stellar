# Customer Discovery Report: 5 Stellar Ecosystem B2B Products

## 1. MoneyGram Ramps

### Product Overview
Cash-to-crypto on/off-ramp service enabling digital wallets and applications to integrate with MoneyGram's global retail network of 500,000+ locations across 200+ countries.

### Target Customers
- **Digital wallet providers** (e.g., Vesseo, Decaf)
- **Cryptocurrency exchanges**
- **Fintech applications**
- **Web3 platforms** seeking fiat on/off-ramps
- **Remittance companies**

### Use Cases
- Users convert physical cash to USDC without bank accounts
- Cash-out digital assets to local currency at physical locations
- Cross-border remittances with instant settlement
- Aid disbursements for humanitarian organizations (UNHCR, IRC)
- Salary payments in stablecoins for unbanked populations

### Value Proposition
- **Global reach**: 500,000+ retail locations worldwide
- **Bankless access**: No bank account required
- **Low fees**: Stellar transaction costs < $0.01
- **Instant settlement**: USDC on Stellar for quick, easy settlement
- **Regulatory compliance**: SEP-24 integration standards

### Key Features
- SEP-24 hosted deposit/withdrawal integration
- Cash-in (physical cash → USDC)
- Cash-out (USDC → local currency)
- Transaction confirmation codes for physical pickup
- Multi-currency support across 170+ countries

### Target Customers
- **Multinational corporations**
- **Online merchants**
- **E-commerce platforms**
- **Financial institutions**
- **B2B service providers**

### Use Cases
- Corporate payments across 150+ countries
- Cross-border B2B transactions
- Multi-currency settlement (fiat + digital)
- Web3 merchant solutions
- Liquidity management at TradFi/CeFi edge

### Value Proposition
- **Unified integration**: Single API across 60+ settlement blockchains
- **Cost reduction**: Lower operational delays and transaction costs
- **Transparency**: Distributed ledger for all transactions
- **Multi-currency**: Support for major fiat currencies globally
- **Interoperability**: Stellar SEP protocols for seamless flows

### Key Features
- Corporate Payments with built-in APS Settlement
- Web3 Merchant Solution
- On-Chain Payments via Stellar blockchain
- Support for 60+ settlement layers (Stellar, Solana, Ethereum, BSC)
- Multi-currency interoperability through SEP protocols

## 3. Arf

### Product Overview
Regulated global liquidity and settlement platform providing short-term, revolving, USDC-based credit lines for cross-border payments to licensed financial institutions.

### Target Customers
- **Licensed money service businesses (MSBs)**
- **Remittance companies**
- **Challenger banks**
- **Neo-banks**
- **Cross-border payment processors**

### Use Cases
- Same-day cross-border settlements
- Elimination of pre-funding requirements
- Liquidity access for new market entrants
- USDC-based credit lines for payment flows
- Counterparty risk reduction

### Value Proposition
- **No pre-funding**: Eliminates capital-intensive correspondent banking
- **Same-day settlement**: vs. 3-5 days via SWIFT
- **On-chain transparency**: All loans and repayments traceable
- **Revolving credit**: Short-term, USDC-based liquidity
- **Market access**: Enables new entrants to scale without large capital

### Key Features
- Short-term revolving USDC credit lines
- Same-day settlement capabilities
- SEP-31 integration for cross-border payments
- On-chain loan tracking and transparency
- $830M+ in USDC loans extended (case study metrics)

## 5. Astera

### Target Customers
- **Small and medium enterprises (SMEs)** in emerging markets
- **Community investors** seeking yield
- **Invoice factoring companies**
- **Supply chain finance providers**
- **DeFi liquidity providers**

### Use Cases
- SME invoice tokenization for immediate funding
- Community-funded USDC liquidity pools
- On-chain credit history building
- 8% APY yield for investors
- Cross-border invoice financing

### Value Proposition
- **Immediate liquidity**: SMEs access capital without waiting 60-90 days
- **On-chain credit history**: Every paid invoice builds reputation
- **Community funding**: Democratized access to capital
- **Smart contract automation**: Escrow, repayment, yield distribution
- **Emerging market focus**: Addresses liquidity gaps in developing economies

### Key Features
- Invoice tokenization via Soroban smart contracts
- USDC liquidity pool funding
- Multi-investor co-funding support
- Credit score tracking on-chain
- 8% APY default interest rate
- Freighter wallet integration
