## Descripción del producto

Skyhitz is a blockchain-powered music platform on Stellar that lets artists upload, share, and monetize music through streaming, tipping, and fractional ownership (NFTs). It connects creators and listeners/investors who transact value around music rights and royalties.

---

## Por qué es candidato para Slice

Skyhitz has creators and buyers/listeners exchanging value around subjective deliverables (music rights, royalties, NFT ownership). Parties may not know each other. Bad-faith incentives include copyright disputes, royalty misallocation, and payment disputes. Meets at least 3 criteria.
