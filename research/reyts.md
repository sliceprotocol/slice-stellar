## Descripción del producto

Reyts is a peer-to-peer currency exchange platform on Stellar that lets users swap fiat currencies with other users (or merchants) through transparent on-chain settlement. It replaces informal WhatsApp-based exchanges with a structured P2P marketplace, including a WhatsApp bot channel for convenience.

---

## Por qué es candidato para Slice

Reyts connects two strangers exchanging money across borders. Funds move only when both sides fulfill their side of the swap. There is strong bad-faith incentive (payment not sent, wrong amount, impersonation scams — explicitly referenced in their marketing). Meets at least 4 criteria: P2P value exchange, fulfillment-based settlement, strangers, and bad-faith risk.
