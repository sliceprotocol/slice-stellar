## Descripción del producto

Choppaddi (formerly FastBuka) is a blockchain-enabled food and grocery delivery platform built on the Stellar network. It connects customers, local food vendors, and delivery riders across Africa with instant payments, multi-asset support (NGNC, USDC, XLM), built-in dispute resolution, and task-based rewards. The platform serves cities in Nigeria and is expanding across Africa.

---

## Por qué es candidato para Slice

Choppaddi is a three-sided marketplace (customers, vendors, riders) where every transaction involves multiple handoffs and potential failure points: wrong orders, late deliveries, missing items, payment disputes, and rider-vendor conflicts. The platform already mentions "built-in dispute resolution" in its SCF pitch, signaling awareness of the problem. This is a high-volume, real-world use case with clear dispute scenarios that Slice could handle.
