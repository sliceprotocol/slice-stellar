## Descripción del producto

Stallion is a decentralized bounty platform on Stellar that connects global Web3 talent with projects. It enables projects to post bounties and contributors to complete tasks and earn rewards through transparent on-chain payments. The platform handles the full lifecycle: bounty creation, work submission, review, and reward release.

---

## Por qué es candidato para Slice

Stallion is a two-sided marketplace (bounty posters and contributors) where value is exchanged for completed work. The core transaction — approving or rejecting a submission — is inherently subjective and prone to disputes. A contributor may claim their work meets requirements while the bounty poster disagrees, or vice versa. This is a textbook use case for Slice's arbitration layer. Meets criteria: P2P between strangers, escrowed funds, subjective deliverables.
